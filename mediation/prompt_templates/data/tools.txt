# One mediation tool per line
I-statements worksheet ('When ___, I feel ___, because ___, I need ___')
Needs vs. requests ladder (separate underlying need from specific ask)
Time-out signal card (agreed pause word before escalation)
Weekly chore rotation chart with explicit ownership
Emotion naming check-in (name the feeling before the argument)
Speaker-listener paraphrase (repeat back before responding)
Fairness scale (each person rates balance 1–10 and explains)
Future-self letter (write what you'd want resolved in 6 months)
